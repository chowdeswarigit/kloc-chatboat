# KLOC General FAQ

## What does KLOC do?
KLOC is an engineering partner focused on commerce engineering, custom software, and technology talent.

## What are KLOC's main divisions?
KLOCOM — Commerce Engineering; KLOCAPPS — Custom Software; KLOCERS — Technology Talent.

## Does KLOC work with Shopify?
Yes. KLOCOM focuses on Shopify and commerce engineering, including Shopify Plus builds, migrations, integrations, and managed services.

## Does KLOC build custom software?
Yes. KLOCAPPS builds custom web and mobile applications, platforms, enterprise integrations, and AI products.

## Does KLOC build mobile applications?
Yes. Mobile application development is a KLOCAPPS capability.

## Does KLOC build AI products?
Yes. AI products are listed under KLOCAPPS, and KLOC describes AI chat/shopping agents as part of agentic commerce.

## Does KLOC provide developers?
Yes. KLOCERS provides senior engineers, delivery pods, and embedded engineering teams.

## Does KLOC provide dedicated development teams?
Yes. Dedicated engineering teams and delivery pods are part of KLOCERS.

## Does KLOC provide Shopify migration services?
Yes. Shopify migrations are listed under KLOCOM.

## Does KLOC provide integrations?
Yes. Integrations are part of the commerce engineering and custom software offerings.

## How does KLOC work with clients?
Discover → Architect → Build → Grow.

## Where is KLOC located?
Indiranagar, Bangalore, India.

## How quickly does KLOC respond?
KLOC states that it responds within one business day.

## Does KLOC provide pricing?
The public knowledge base does not contain standardized pricing. The chatbot must not invent pricing.
