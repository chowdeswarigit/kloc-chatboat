# How KLOC Works

KLOC follows a four-stage delivery approach.

## 01 — Discover
Understand requirements, dependencies, risks, and scope.

## 02 — Architect
Establish technical architecture before implementation, including architecture diagrams, integration contracts, migration runbooks, and technical planning.

## 03 — Build
Develop through working increments, preview environments, demonstrations, development, testing, and launch support.

## 04 — Grow
Continue support after launch through managed services, experimentation, AI readiness, architecture review, and ongoing engineering support.
