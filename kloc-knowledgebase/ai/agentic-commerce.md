# KLOC — AI and Agentic Commerce

KLOC positions AI as part of its commerce engineering capabilities.

## Agentic Commerce
KLOC describes a shift in ecommerce where AI agents can participate in product discovery and shopping experiences.

## AI Visibility
KLOC describes an AI visibility capability focused on helping businesses be discovered and cited by AI systems such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.

## Catalog Enrichment
KLOC describes catalog enrichment involving product attributes, product content, A+ content, and structured product data.

## AI Chat and Shopping Agents
KLOC describes AI chat and shopping agents for conversational product discovery, AI-powered shopping assistance, product recommendation, and customer interactions.

Do not claim a specific AI solution was implemented for a customer unless verified knowledge supports it.
